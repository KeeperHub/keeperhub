# Hub

The Hub is KeeperHub's directory of community-shared workflow templates. Use it to discover automations other users have built, copy them into your workspace, and share your own.

Open the Hub from the **Hub** entry in the left sidebar.

## Browsing templates

The Hub lists every public workflow other users have shared. Each template shows its name, description, and the nodes it uses, so you can see at a glance what it automates before importing.

## Duplicating a template

To use a template:

1. Open the Hub from the left sidebar
2. Select a workflow template
3. Click **Duplicate** to copy it into your workspace

The copy lands in your account with:

- A unique name appended with `(Copy)` (e.g. `Aave V3 Health Factor Guardian (Copy)`).
- Private visibility, so it's only visible to you until you choose to share it.
- All node configurations preserved.
- Integration credentials cleared, so you can attach your own [Connections](/workflows/creating#managing-connections) before running.

You can also duplicate any public workflow you're viewing by clicking the **Duplicate** button in the canvas toolbar, without going through the Hub list.

## Sharing your own workflow

Any workflow you set to public visibility appears in the Hub for other users to discover and duplicate. Set visibility from the workflow's **Properties** panel on the right of the canvas.

When other users duplicate your workflow, your credentials are not copied with it: they have to attach their own [Connections](/workflows/creating#managing-connections) before they can run the duplicated copy.
